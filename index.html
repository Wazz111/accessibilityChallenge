01 / THE MISSION

The future is inclusive.

HACKTRENT × COMMUNITY

Great technology doesn't leave people behind.

HackTrent brings builders together to learn, experiment, and turn ideas into real experiences. The Accessible Website Challenge asks us to make a shared website easier to navigate, read, and use.

That includes keyboard-only navigation, thoughtful visual contrast, clear forms, semantic HTML, and compatibility with assistive technologies.

02 / THE STANDARD

Built for every interaction.

Five areas. One goal: an experience people can actually use.

01 / INPUT⌘

25 / 100 points

Keyboard navigation & focus

Everything works with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, and Space — with visible focus at every step.

02 / DESIGN◉

20 / 100 points

Visual accessibility

Readable type, clear contrast, responsive layouts, and adjustable text size.

03 / STRUCTURE{ }

20 / 100 points

Semantic HTML & screen readers

Meaningful landmarks, headings, labels, and image descriptions.

04 / ACTION↗

15 / 100 points

Forms & interaction

Clear input labels, useful errors, status messages, and accessible controls.

05 / QUALITY✓

20 / 100 points

Testing & technical quality

Keyboard checks, automated audits, and real-world usability testing.
